01 — ABOUT VIZDEV

Small studio.
Big curiosity.

VIZDEV is an independent digital product studio focused on imagining, developing and publishing software experiences for a connected world.

We bring ideas to life across different categories and audiences. Our work spans social and community experiences, games, kids’ learning applications, productivity tools and other digital products.

Each product is approached with a focus on thoughtful design, practical usefulness and a reliable experience for the people who use it.
